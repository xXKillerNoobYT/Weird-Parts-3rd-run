## One operation path for callers

UI and local MCP submit the same typed commands. A caller supplies the target, intended patch, observed causal heads, and a retry identity. An authenticated local session supplies the company, user, device, and permissions. A caller cannot substitute an author ID.

```text
commands.patchJobLine(session, lineId, observedHeads, patch, requestId)
    -> committed operation and durable local receipt, or explicit refusal
commands.resolveConflict(session, issueId, observedHeads, answer, requestId)
    -> attributed resolution operation, or explicit refusal
sync.syncNow(authenticatedPeer)
    -> both-direction record, conflict, pending-media, and checkpoint results
```

The command service owns permission and domain validation. The replication store owns atomic operation, projection, conflict, and receipt persistence. The transport owns bounded authenticated delivery. A radio adapter does not decide what an operation means. Whole-shop replacement and backup remain separate commands.

`requestId` is the permanent random operation ID, allocated once for a user action and retained until its durable receipt is known. Its namespace includes the company and originating installation generation. The commit binds that ID, authenticated user/session, canonical command commitment, resulting operation bytes, and original receipt atomically. Retrying a committed action returns that operation and receipt. Reusing its ID with different content or actor is refused, rather than allocating another operation.

At c998, `app/lib/data/app_database.dart` declares schema 2 and no membership, operation, receipt, or conflict tables. `JobsDao.updateJobLine` rewrites scalar fields and increments a local revision. `SyncColumns` preserves permanent IDs and tombstones, but it does not establish causal history or the last human writer. References are text columns without enforced SQLite foreign keys. Stage 2 must validate company-scoped references explicitly.

## Company, installation, and user authority

A company has a permanent random ID and a pinned public authority identity. Shops belong to one company. Catalog records are company-wide. A job or later location record can have additional shop scope. A device belongs to exactly one company and has a distinct installation ID and cryptographic key pair. A company ID, radio name, PIN, matching code, or copied public grant is never proof of membership.

Create Company bootstraps its first administrator and authority on that installation. Join Existing Company generates a new local installation key and pairs with an enrolled device that has `enrollDevice` authority. An approver signs a grant for that exact public key, company, permitted shops, capabilities, authority generation, and grant ID. Delegation cannot exceed the issuer's scope. Enrollment retries return the existing grant for the same approved request and cannot widen it. A successful grant authorizes the company network, rather than only the pairing partner. Each session proves possession and binds both grants, company scope, protocol, and ephemeral session keys before protected metadata is exchanged.

Administrator authority is an explicit user capability plus an enrolled approver device, not possession of today's catalog PIN. Removing devices and assigning administrators require their declared capabilities. Signed revocation evidence is monotonic. Device key rotation preserves the device record only through an authorized continuity proof. Reset creates a new untrusted installation and requires enrollment to rejoin the original company.

Concurrent divergent administrative changes at the same authority generation form a held authority fork. Devices do not union granted permissions or select a branch by arrival order. Revocations still constrain authority, and affected grants, roles, and privileged operations remain unavailable until an attributable authorized reconciliation covers the fork. An unsupported or incomplete authority chain cannot provision credentials or disclose protected data.

Company authority recovery is separate from a shop backup. Create Company supports a separately encrypted administrator recovery package and a second authorized administrator. Losing all authorized administrators and the recovery package cannot be repaired by trusting a company name or restored database. Recovery rotates authority under the registered recovery policy and preserves revocation history. Its UI, crypto, storage, and recovery proof are required before claiming enrollment complete.

Company creation chooses an explicit, signed offline policy. `localContinuity` permits offline work and known-authority sessions without an elapsed-age cutoff, shows the last authority update, and rejects every revocation already known locally. `boundedAge` requires a fresh authorized renewal after the configured duration and fails closed when freshness cannot be established. There is no silent default to infinite validity and no claim that a disconnected peer knows unseen revocations. The first profile fits the user's fully offline workflow. The second is a deliberate availability tradeoff. Native clock rollback and restart tests precede enabling the second profile.

A receiver that learns an author's credential is revoked re-evaluates every affected operation, including operations it admitted before learning the revocation. Trustworthy authenticated authority evidence may prove an operation valid under an explicit revocation cutoff. In that case, both delivery orders retain its authorized status. Without that evidence, both orders place it in the same attributable `authorityReview` state for an authorized manager's adoption or refusal. Do not use a device clock or a claimed creation time as cutoff evidence. Keep the immutable operation, original author, reported physical facts, and prior decision history visible. Do not silently delete or rewrite them. Suspend any unreviewed effect from the authoritative projection while preserving it as an unaccepted report. Manager adoption creates a new attributed operation; refusal records the reason and retains the reported facts as unaccepted history. A carrier's valid current membership cannot erase the original author's revocation. A known-revoked connecting device receives no protected company data.

Before enabling this behavior, test two replicas with the same authored operation and authenticated revocation delivered in both orders. After exchange, restart, and replay, both replicas must show the same authority-review status, affected projection, provenance, and pending receipts. After authorized manager adoption or refusal, they must converge on the same decision and history. Also test a valid authenticated cutoff proof and reject claimed-time-only and tampered cutoff evidence. These proofs have not run.

Protected local security state retains known grant, device, and user revocations, credential and permission-version floors, and authority generations. These are separate signed records and monotonic version floors, not an assumption that one scalar epoch encodes every change. Business restore cannot remove them. Recipient delivery/apply evidence is keyed by company, recipient installation, receiving-store generation, and operation commitment.

Private installation keys, active sessions, protected user secrets, and the minimum known authority generation live outside business backup/replication. Mac Keychain and Windows user-scoped protected storage are candidate adapters, not tested implementation. Their official documentation describes [Apple key storage](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/Security/Conceptual/cryptoservices/KeyManagementAPIs/KeyManagementAPIs.html) and [Windows protection scope](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/seccrypto/example-c-program-using-cryptprotectdata). Keychain synchronization must be disabled for installation credentials. No plaintext credential file fallback is allowed.

Users and their permissions have company-scoped permanent IDs. Versioned user authentication provisioning is encrypted for authorized recipient installations over a mutually authenticated channel. Generic business envelopes and public user profiles contain no raw PIN or verifier. Local verification material is protected with the installation's secure-storage key and is not a new device grant. A newly created user or rotated PIN works on another device only after authorized provisioning arrives. User revocation and credential versions cannot roll back through shop restore.

Each authored operation includes a verifiable user/session authorization assertion bound to its immutable content commitment, company, user, device, grant, credential/permission versions, and permitted action/scope. The remote receiver verifies that assertion and the attesting device's authority under its current known security state and selected offline policy. An unsigned author field or a valid carrier session cannot substitute for this proof. Secret authentication material never travels inside that assertion.

The current salted SHA-256 catalog PIN verifier is a legacy gate, not a portable authentication scheme. Keep it until a reviewed per-user migration preserves access and rollback. Portable authentication uses a versioned password-hardening profile, per-user salt, protected provisioning, persisted attempt limits, session expiry, and administrator reset. A short PIN cannot resist unlimited offline guessing merely because it is hashed. Exact hardening parameters and platform key custody require their focused security review and measurement before authentication code is enabled. Ephemeral synthetic development PINs are runtime inputs for isolated local fixtures only. Never place raw credentials, PINs, verifiers, or private keys in committed fixtures, shared records, logs, or evidence. A trusted enrolled device attests the user session; this does not prove which human physically held a compromised shared device.

## Immutable operations with typed projections

Select the immutable operation store and transactional outgoing-change path, with supported typed projections. The alternatives and their measured small-fixture observations appear in the storage comparison receipt. Strengthened row-state can preserve concurrent heads and reject replay. It does not retain a superseded author's intermediate operation bytes unless a separate historical store is added. Original actions and detailed attribution are requirements here, not optional debugging logs.

Each operation contains its format, company, entity kind and permanent ID, entity contract version, original device and user, installation generation, random operation ID, causal parent IDs, editing units, atomic group, dependencies, authorization evidence, payload bytes, and authenticated content digest. Random operation IDs avoid reusing an archived counter after restore. The authenticated canonical encoding and signature suite are separate protocol choices requiring review before exchanging real credentials. No peer-supplied executable adapter is allowed.

A local transaction commits the validated projection, original immutable operation, audit, and outgoing eligibility together. Incoming storage validates bounded framing, scope, authorization, hash, versions, values, references, and dependencies before domain application. Quantity values must be finite and satisfy the registered quantity/unit rules. Application, deduplication evidence, conflict alternatives, and durable receipt commit together. Same ID and authenticated bytes returns the saved outcome. Same ID and different bytes is an integrity refusal. A crash after commit but before response returns the persisted receipt on retry. An interrupted uncommitted batch produces no success receipt.

Receipt types distinguish custody, semantic apply, conflict/dependency deferral, intended-shop acceptance, and human acknowledgment. Later status transitions are durable linked facts. Every receipt that can survive its session or be relayed binds an authenticated issuer identity and authority, company, operation content commitment, recipient installation, receiving-store generation, and exact status. Verify the issuer's authority and receipt scope after the original session ends. An authenticated session with the immediate carrier alone proves neither who issued a durable receipt nor whether its status is true. The receipt encoding and signature suite remain undecided.

A custody receipt never suppresses missing semantic application. Relay preserves original bytes, authors, scope, and operation IDs. It does not make the carrier the author or broaden the carrier's permissions. Receipt status does not authorize deletion or pruning of operations; retention and pruning require their own admitted policy and checkpoint proof.

Before enabling durable or relayed receipts, verify that valid issuer-authenticated statuses survive relay, restart, and duplicate delivery. Forged issuers and tampered company, operation commitment, recipient installation, store generation, status, or transition links must fail. Replayed valid receipts must not change status for a different operation or store generation. Immediate-session authentication alone must not make a fabricated acceptance authoritative. These proofs have not run.

The first semantic implementation supports registered known contracts only. Unsupported required identity, authentication, envelope, or entity rules reject before protected exchange or application. Optional unknown-byte carriage stays disabled until its understood authorization envelope, quotas, durable custody receipts, backup preservation, and replay tests pass. An older UI therefore does not imply universal future-format compatibility. Photos have portable asset IDs and hashes with per-device pending/available state, separate from record apply.

## Causality, conflicts, and deletion

Causal parents identify the versions actually observed by the editor. Operations on independent editing units may combine only when the resulting domain state passes validation. Coupled fields such as quantity and unit form one editing unit. The wall clock never selects a winner. Independent business actions retain distinct operation IDs and accumulate their effects once. Similar text or quantities do not make separately authored requests duplicates.

For the same complete candidate set, conflict identity is the digest of company, entity, editing unit, normalized common-base frontier, and sorted competing operation IDs. The frontier contains every maximal shared causal ancestor, sorted by stable identity. Missing ancestry defers this calculation. For concurrent resolutions R1 and R2 that both depend on A and B, the common frontier is the set A and B, not an arrival-selected single base. Arrival order and relay path cannot create another issue for the same set. A later unseen alternative extends the issue history with a linked candidate-set identity. Both attributed versions remain readable. Questions target the original editors, and their answers remain history. An authorized manager can resolve disagreement.

A resolution references every observed competing head and creates a new operation. Concurrent managers' differing resolutions remain concurrent and create a visible linked issue. Equivalent resolutions can share a displayed value while retaining both authored operations. A late alternative is never silently covered by an earlier answer. Edit-versus-delete retains both alternatives and a deletion barrier. Ordinary stale edits cannot restore the row. An explicit authorized restore is a separate operation.

The first foundation performs no tombstone or acknowledged-history garbage collection. Bounded transfer and local resource refusal keep exhaustion visible. Compaction requires a separately reviewed participant-retirement and admitted-baseline protocol. A stale or restored peer cannot send past that boundary without reconciliation. Last-known stock time, device receipt time, and transfer time remain different observations.

## Migration and the first implementation slice

Database schema, backup format, transport envelope, entity contract, and app UI versions are independent. No schema migration runs in this documentation milestone. The first data migration preserves all current UUIDs and relationships, records an honest legacy baseline without inventing authors, and refuses ambiguous company adoption. Existing installed apps, schema-2 fixtures, encrypted recovery evidence, and original runtimes remain rollback assets.

Before business restore, pause domain mutations and synchronization, then durably retain every locally committed operation and required asset absent from the selected snapshot. This includes unacknowledged operations for which this device holds the only copy. A checked recovery journal retains their original bytes, commitments, provenance, and pending state before activation. Preservation failure aborts restore without replacing the live store. After activation, reconcile these operations against the restored baseline and current authority, preserving unsupported or conflicting changes visibly instead of erasing or blindly replaying them. The preservation journal and assets remain until their recovery is admitted.

Business restore uses a new receiving-store generation and invalidates delivery assumptions for that receiving generation. Peers reconcile missing operations rather than treating old delivery receipts as proof that restored data still exists. Installation identity and known security floors remain outside the restored business snapshot. Unknown local identity continuity requires re-enrollment. Downgrade never opens an unsupported database or discards any committed operation, acknowledged or pending. Rollback after shared effects requires a compatible binary or authorized compensating operations, not replacement with a stale snapshot.

Secure storage and SQLite do not share a transaction. Installation activation therefore has persisted `unjoined`, `pending`, `active`, and `needsRecovery` states. A pending activation records its key reference and intended generation before an atomic database binding is admitted. Restart reconciles both stores and activates only a matching key proof, binding, and nonrollback authority floor. Missing, conflicting, or restored bindings fail closed. The same recovery journal covers business-restore activation. No pending state authorizes company data.

The first Stage 2 increment is the local installation/security binding and its restore boundary. It adds typed company/shop/user/device references, distinct installation keys, a protected key reference, activation recovery, and nonrollback authority/store generations. It proves restart identity continuity, preservation through same-install business restore, no enrollment or sender-key cloning through foreign restore, and fail-closed interrupted activation. Mac owns its native secure-storage adapter and proof. Windows owns its adapter, integration, and proof. The shared contract stays in Dart. No transport, portable-PIN UI, or broad editor migration joins this increment.

The next Stage 2 increment adds one company-scoped `job.line.patch@1` vertical slice. It includes verified membership and permission facts, immutable operations, projection, receipt, conflict persistence, and an additive isolated migration. Both local edit and incoming apply use one transaction. Other editors and networking remain unchanged until explicitly migrated. A public command accepts only a verified session supplied by the authorization service. Synthetic grant fixtures are test inputs, not a production bypass or enrollment proof.

Acceptance for that command slice uses two isolated Drift stores. Verify projection/outgoing atomicity, denial of wrong company/shop/user capabilities, exact replay receipts, different-byte ID refusal, same-field and edit/delete alternatives, restart, missing references, unsupported contracts, rollback, and a fault before commit. The separately pending review clarifications also require opposite-order revocation delivery with replay, restart, and manager resolution, plus valid, forged, tampered, and cross-generation durable receipt cases before those behaviors can be enabled. None of these additional proofs has run. Native enrollment/provisioning, portable sign-in, and real LAN exchange need their own implementation and actual-device proof before the app exposes dependable company sync. This ordering implements the target in reversible units without treating later warehouse, Bluetooth, Notebook, AI, or cloud modules as current blockers.
